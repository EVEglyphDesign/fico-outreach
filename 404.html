Datasphere History Reconstitution

EVEglyphDesign · sovereign reference model for reconstituting SAP profitability history when margin analysis was never activated in the Universal Journal.

A prior systems integrator migrated ECC → S/4 without activating margin analysis in ACDOCA. SAP guidance forbids retroactive population. This blueprint reconstitutes the historical profitability record in Datasphere against the underlying journal-line evidence — a portable, semantic-layer derivation rather than a locked-in re-platform.
